# Toolbar

Bar buttons, grouped the way iOS 26 groups them on one Liquid Glass capsule, for the [Navigation Bar](/dolan/navigation-bar) and for a bar of actions along the bottom of the screen.

<Go example="ui-navigation-bar" defaultFile="src/app/screens.ts" />

## Installation

```bash
npx @blotch/dolan add toolbar
```

```typescript
import {
  UiBarScroll,
  UiToolbar,
  UiToolbarGroup,
  UiToolbarItem,
  UiToolbarSpacer,
} from '../components/ui/toolbar';
```

:::tip
This also adds [Glass](/dolan/glass), which draws the items, and [Icon](/dolan/icon), whose icons they show.
:::

## Usage

Put a `<ui-toolbar>` after the screen's `<scroll-view>`, and `ui-bar-scroll` on the scroll view so the content ends above it:

```angular-html
<scroll-view ui-bar-scroll scroll-orientation="vertical" class="h-full w-full">
  …
</scroll-view>

<ui-toolbar>
  <ui-toolbar-group>
    <ui-toolbar-item
      icon="list-checks"
      label="Checklist"
      (pressed)="addChecklist()"
    />
    <ui-toolbar-item icon="camera" label="Camera" (pressed)="addPhoto()" />
  </ui-toolbar-group>
  <ui-toolbar-spacer />
  <ui-toolbar-item icon="pencil" label="New note" (pressed)="compose()" />
</ui-toolbar>
```

- **The toolbar floats over the content** at the bottom of the screen, above the home indicator. Content scrolls beneath it, and on iOS 26 fades out as it does.
- **Items spread across it.** A `<ui-toolbar-spacer>` takes the free space instead, pushing what follows it to the end.
- **Every item needs a `label`.** It's what a screen reader says, and what the item shows without an icon.

### What each platform draws

| Platform                                   | Toolbar                                                                                                               | Items                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| iOS 26 and later                           | No bar: the items float in Liquid Glass                                                                               | Glass circles and capsules; a group shares one capsule            |
| iOS 15 to 25                               | Edge to edge, on the bars' chrome material with a hairline while content continues beneath it, clear at the end of it | Plain bar buttons in the tint color, which fade while pressed     |
| Android                                    | Material 3 Expressive's docked toolbar, on the surface container                                                      | Material icon buttons with a state layer; labels are text buttons |
| Android, with `provideGlass()`             | As on iOS 26, drawn with Android's blur                                                                               | As on iOS 26                                                      |
| [Web preview](/guide/glossary#web-preview) | As on iOS 26, drawn with WebGL                                                                                        | As on iOS 26                                                      |

### Groups

`<ui-toolbar-group>` puts related items on one glass capsule, as iOS 26 groups a bar's items. Before iOS 26 and on Android it only keeps them together.

```angular-html
<ui-toolbar-group>
  <ui-toolbar-item icon="share-2" label="Share" (pressed)="share()" />
  <ui-toolbar-item icon="heart" label="Favorite" (pressed)="favorite()" />
</ui-toolbar-group>
```

- **An item on its own** is a glass circle for an icon, or a capsule for a label.
- **Keep labels apart from icons.** Side by side on one capsule, they read as one button.
- **Aim for three groups at most** in a bar, as Apple suggests.

### Prominent items

`prominent` marks the screen's key action, such as Done: tinted glass on iOS 26, a bold label before it, and a filled button on Android. Give it a place of its own at the trailing end; in a group it's a plain item.

```angular-html
<ui-toolbar-item label="Done" prominent (pressed)="save()" />
```

### Back items

`back` draws the platform's Back button: a glass circle with a chevron on iOS 26, the chevron and `label` before it, and Material's arrow on Android. The [Navigation Bar](/dolan/navigation-bar#the-back-button) shows one by itself; use this one for a Back button of your own.

### The scroll view

`ui-bar-scroll` on a `<scroll-view>` is what connects the content to the bars over it:

- **It makes room** for the navigation bar and the status bar at the top, and the toolbar and the home indicator at the bottom.
- **It draws the large title** of a navigation bar with `largeTitle`.
- **It fades content out** under glass bars, iOS 26's scroll edge effect. Android's scroll view takes no mask, so there the glass bars fade content to the theme's `background` color themselves.
- **It tells the bars where the content is**: the frosted bars turn opaque, and Material's top app bar changes color, as content scrolls under them.

It reads the scroll view's events itself rather than through a template listener, so it updates the bars only when they change. Only a `<scroll-view>` takes it; a `<list>` can't hold the spacers.

## API

### UiToolbarItem

| Input       | Type                    | Default     | Description                                                                      |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `label`     | `string`                | —           | Required. What the item does: shown without an icon, and read by screen readers. |
| `icon`      | `IconName \| undefined` | `undefined` | The [Icon](/dolan/icon) shown instead of the label.                              |
| `prominent` | `boolean`               | `false`     | The screen's key action, tinted, on its own.                                     |
| `back`      | `boolean`               | `false`     | The platform's Back button, with `label` as the previous screen's title.         |
| `disabled`  | `boolean`               | `false`     | Dims the item and ignores taps.                                                  |

| Output    | Type   | Description                           |
| --------- | ------ | ------------------------------------- |
| `pressed` | `void` | Emits on a tap, unless it's disabled. |

### UiToolbar, UiToolbarGroup and UiToolbarSpacer

| Component         | Selector            | Inputs  | Description                                                           |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `UiToolbar`       | `ui-toolbar`        | `class` | The bar along the bottom, which places itself at its parent's bottom. |
| `UiToolbarGroup`  | `ui-toolbar-group`  | `class` | Items sharing one glass capsule.                                      |
| `UiToolbarSpacer` | `ui-toolbar-spacer` | —       | Flexible space between items.                                         |

### UiBarScroll and UiBarState

| Name          | Kind                                    | Description                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `UiBarScroll` | Component, `scroll-view[ui-bar-scroll]` | Makes room for the bars, draws the large title, fades content under glass bars and tells the bars where the content is.                                                    |
| `UiBarState`  | Root service                            | What the bars and the scroll view tell each other. Provide one on a component to give its bars their own (see [Two bars at once](/dolan/navigation-bar#two-bars-at-once)). |

## Accessibility

- **Each item is one button,** named by its `label`. A disabled item also has the `disabled` trait.
- **Reduce Motion:** the web preview's glass stops swelling under the finger, and the item shows a highlight instead. iOS calms its own glass.
- **Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast** are handled by the [glass](/dolan/glass#accessibility).
