# Signal Forms

A contact form built with Angular's signal forms, with required and email validation on the UI components' inputs. Look here to wire `@angular/forms/signals` into an AngularLynx app.

<Go example="forms" defaultFile="src/app/app.ts" />

## What it shows

- **`form()`** — builds the form from a `signal` model, with `required()` and `email()` rules in its schema. See [Forms](/guide/forms).
- **`[formField]`** — binds each [Input](/dolan/input) and [Textarea](/dolan/textarea) to its field, through the `FormField` directive.
- **Field state** — `touched()` and `errors()` decide when each error message appears.
- **`submit()`** — marks every field as touched, then runs the callback only if the form is valid.
- **UI components** — the inputs and the [Button](/dolan/button) come from the [UI components](/dolan/).
